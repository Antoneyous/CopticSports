$100

Player Fee

A non-refundable $100 covers a personalized dry-fit TCBL jersey and guarantees at least eight full-length games. Coaches and volunteers don’t register here. Email coptichoops@gmail.com instead.

Extra jerseys $20
80 Spots

Player Cap

The first 72 spots sold out in under an hour, so we've opened a one-time expansion to 80 players: 8 more spots, open until they're filled. When the cap is reached, you can still be part of the season as a coach or volunteer by emailing coptichoops@gmail.com.

Who Can Play

Men ages 18 and up from any Coptic Orthodox church in the Tampa Bay area. We are sanctioned under the Coptic Orthodox Diocese of the Southern United States and are not tied to a single parish. Your home church has no bearing on your team.

How Teams Are Formed

The league committee selects eight captains from the registration pool. Captains then take turns picking teammates at a private draft, landing each roster at 8–10 players.

Season Format

Eleven Sundays, 3:30–8:00 PM, at St. Reweis and St. Verena, with a bye week on Feb 21. Games are 40 minutes — two 20-minute halves — with professional referees. Season 5 runs Jan 3 through the Finals on Mar 14, 2027.
