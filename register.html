Player Cap

Season 5 is capped at 72 players to protect the quality of the league and respect our facilities, referees, and church schedules. Register early — when the cap is reached, you can still join as a coach or volunteer.

Who Can Play

Men ages 18 and up from any Coptic Orthodox church in the Tampa Bay area. We are sanctioned under the Coptic Orthodox Diocese of the Southern United States and are not tied to a single parish. Your home church has no bearing on your team.

How Teams Are Formed

The league committee selects eight captains from the registration pool. Captains then take turns picking teammates at a private draft, landing each roster at 8–10 players.

Season Format

Eleven Sundays, 3:30–8:00 PM, alternating between St. Verena and St. Reweis, with a bye week on Feb 21. Games are 40 minutes — two 20-minute halves — with professional referees. Season 5 runs Jan 3 through the Finals on Mar 14, 2027.
